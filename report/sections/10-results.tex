\section{Contest results}
\label{sec:results}

We won the contest with a 5--1 record (Table~\ref{tab:rounds}). The competition score is $50\,P/M + 50$, where $P$ is our points in the Swiss tournament (1 per win) and $M$ is the most points any team received. As the winners we had the most points, so $P = M = 5$ and the score is 100.

Our one loss, in the final round, came from the timed advance after the crest. We reached the mesa first and ended up under the opponent, as designed, but the robot stopped short of where it usually did. The advance runs for a set time rather than a measured distance, so where it ends varies from run to run with track slip and the vibration of a robot held together with tape and hand-cut side guards (Section~\ref{sec:layout}). That run it fell short, the opponent's farthest point was closer to the mesa center than ours, and we lost on that rule.
