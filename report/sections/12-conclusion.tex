\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Our objective was to reach the mesa first and keep the opponent from displacing us for the rest of the \qty{30}{s}. Three decisions mattered most: a low wedge with forks that lets an opponent drive up and over us, larger motors at the best available gear ratio, and a pitch estimate that fuses the gyro and accelerometer so the robot can end its climb and hold its place on the mesa. The robot won the contest with a 5--1 record, and four of the five wins resulted from ground game. Its one loss came from the timed advance onto the mesa, which left the robot short. If we built it again, we would add the absolute position reference we did not have time to build (Section~\ref{sec:lessons}).
