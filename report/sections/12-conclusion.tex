\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Our objective was to reach the mesa first and keep the opponent from displacing us for the rest of the \qty{30}{s}. Three decisions mattered most: a low wedge with forks that neutralizes an opponents' drive force, larger motors at an optimal gear ratio, and a pitch estimate that fuses the gyro and accelerometer and ends the climb at the crest. The robot won the contest with a 5--1 record, and its one loss came from a timed advance that fell short of the crest. If we built it again, we would measure position instead of timing the advance.
