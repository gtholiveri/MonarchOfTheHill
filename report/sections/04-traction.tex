\section{Traction}
\label{sec:traction}

Section~\ref{sec:drivetrain} showed that traction caps how much of the motors' torque the robot can use, so the track surface is the most leveraged part of the drivetrain.

The stock tracks are hard plastic links. \tbd{Describe what we saw with them on the ramp.} We tried tennis grip tape on the tracks for more contact area, but the tape slipped off because of the angle the tracks sit at. The final surface is strips of 80 grit sandpaper taped onto the stock tracks (Fig.~\ref{fig:tracks}).

We tested each surface \tbd{three times at each of three normal forces, adding \_\_\_ g of mass between sets} (Table~\ref{tab:friction}).
